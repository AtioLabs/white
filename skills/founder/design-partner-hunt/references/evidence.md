# Evidence behind the defaults (researched 2026-11-05)

Mostly practitioner and vendor guides, not hard data. Re-check before quoting to the founder.

- Founder-led selling, and LinkedIn posts bringing in the first customer: Haptik's Aakrit Vaish, https://saasclub.io/podcast/first-customers/
- Indian GTM: 5 paying design partners at a ₹50k ACV minimum; the founder closes 10 before hiring sales. https://www.startupbricks.in/blog/b2b-saas-gtm-playbook-india-arr/
- Indian founders struggle to tell whether an enterprise has budget. https://blume.vc/commentaries/the-rough-guide-to-building-an-enterprise-saas-dhandha-in-india
- Inbound grows by around month 9, so outbound comes first. https://preevee.in/blog/saas-lead-generation-india
- Cohort size 3–15; channel order; screening; written commitment; 90–120 day cap.
